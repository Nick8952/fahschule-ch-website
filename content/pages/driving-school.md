## Kontrollfahrt – foreign driver's licence

You should exchange your foreign driver's licence within one year of the date of entry into Switzerland. Early applications are recommended, as formalities might extend the waiting period in some cases. The driving authorisation with the foreign licence expires after one year – it is important that you do not drive in Switzerland after the exchange period expires.

You can exchange the foreign licence at any time after this year has expired. If your registration took place after the first year of residence in Switzerland, you must pass the driving examination («Kontrollfahrt») within three months. The examination is performed with automatic transmission; with the Swiss licence you may also drive cars with manual transmission.

**Important:** You cannot repeat this practical exam. If you fail, you have to start all over again. Driving licences obtained abroad are only accepted if you lived there for at least 12 months, proven with official documents.
