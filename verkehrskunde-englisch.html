Traffic Awareness Course (VKU) – English

Fahrschule CH offers monthly VKU English intensive courses in Zurich.

Traffic theory covers the theoretical basics of driving a vehicle. Traffic awareness is trained on the basis of hazard theory. The aim is to avoid risks and accidents in road traffic.

CHF 160

Course fee (cash)

If desired: get a voucher for a free driving lesson at Fahrschule CH.

Requirements

Valid category A, A1 or B learner's driving licence. Missed courses can be repeated monthly.

Bring along

Learner's permit.

Course location

Fahrschulcenter Albisriederplatz, Badenerstrasse 333, 8003 Zürich – 2nd floor above the „Coop“ supermarket at Albisriederplatz. Tram 2 and 3; bus 33, 72 and 83.
